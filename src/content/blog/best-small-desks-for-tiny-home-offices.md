## What to look for in a small desk

- **Width under 40"** — anything bigger eats into walking space in a small room
- **Wall-mounted or floating options** if you need the floor clear for other furniture
- **Built-in cable management** — small rooms make visible cable mess more obvious
- **Depth over 20"** — narrow desks save width but a monitor plus keyboard still needs real depth

**Does a small desk mean I can't fit a dual-monitor setup?**

**What's the minimum width for a comfortable typing position?**
Around 30 inches is the practical floor for keyboard plus mouse without feeling cramped.
